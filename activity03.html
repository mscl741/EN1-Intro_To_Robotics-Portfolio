For this assignment, I asked an AI chatbot for instructions on how to build a paper airplane. The instructions and the finished paper airplane are shown below.

AI-Generated Instructions

How to Fold the Paper Airplane

  1. Start with a standard sheet of paper (8.5 x 11) laid flat in portrait orientation.
  2. Fold it in half lengthwise, crease it sharply, then unfold so you have a center line.
  3. Take the top two corners and fold them in so they meet at the center line, forming a point like a house roof.
  4. Fold the new slanted edges in again toward the center line, making the nose narrower and pointier.
  5. Fold the whole thing in half along the original center crease, with the folds on the outside.
  6. Turn the plane so the folded edge faces down. Fold one wing down so its top edge lines up with the bottom edge of the body. Flip it over and repeat on the other side so the wings match.
  7. Open the wings out flat so they sit level, with a slight upward tilt.
  8. Check that the wings are symmetrical, since uneven wings are the most common reason for a plane that spirals.
