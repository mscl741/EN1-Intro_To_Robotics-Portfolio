Project 3: Object Redesign

Task: Create or Redesign an Object

Each team received an Object card with an object to redesign and a Format card describing the qualities the redesign should have. Our Object card assigned us glasses, and our Format card asked us to create something light, wispy, and floating.

Our Redesign: Float Frames

Float Frames are glasses made from an inflatable material. When the user presses a button, the frames inflate with helium or air and float in the air while the user handles other tasks.

Client Need

Sometimes there is no safe place to put glasses. Float Frames provide an alternative that frees the user's hands and makes it easier to complete a task without setting the glasses down.

Development

The frame would need to be made from an inflatable material such as rubber or latex. Circuits inside the glasses would control the inflation and deflation of the frames.

Potential Limitations

  • Using helium could make the design expensive.
  • The glasses could float away and be difficult to find again.
  • The user might have difficulty seeing certain colors while the glasses are floating away from their face.
