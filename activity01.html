Project 2: Drink Coaster

Design Brief: Create a Coaster Design for a Client

  1. Identify interview questions to assist with doing design. Use Generative AI to help with brainstorming!
  2. Pick a client.
  3. Use questions to interview the client, learn information, and develop design criteria. Use Generative AI to simulate the interview.
  4. Based on research, design a new coaster. Use Generative AI to help design and visualize your ideas!

Client Interview

My teammate and I chose Satoru Gojo as our client. We generated these questions to learn about the client and guide the coaster design:

  • What is this person's job?
  • Where does this person live?
  • What does this person do in their free time?
  • Which design style best reflects their personal space: minimalist/clean, rustic/earthy, vintage/art deco, industrial/modern, or eclectic/maximalist?
  • If someone looked at this coaster on a coffee table without knowing it was theirs, what immediate vibe should it communicate about the room's owner?
  • Is there a specific quote, coordinates, date, monogram, or inside joke that holds deep personal significance for them?
  • What design language is in their home?
  • What do they drink?
  • Where do they drink the most?
  • Do they move around the house a lot or stay stationary?
  • What color palette does their home use?

Client Responses and Design Criteria

  1. Job: Special Grade Jujutsu Sorcerer and instructor at Tokyo Prefectural Jujutsu High School.
  2. Location: Tokyo, Japan.
  3. Free time and hobbies: Pastry and sweet hunting, shopping, and social disruption.
  4. Personal design style: Minimalist/clean with modern luxury.
  5. Immediate coaster vibe: Effortless authority and quiet precision.
  6. Significant detail: The signature quote, "Throughout heaven and earth, I alone am the honored one."
  7. Home design language: Sharp architectural geometry, floating or low-profile furniture, floor-to-ceiling glass, and raw, polished textures.
  8. Beverage preferences: Canned sweet coffee, milk teas, fruit syrups, high-grade iced matcha, or green tea paired with traditional sweets.
  9. Color palette: Deep obsidian black, stark pure white, and charcoal gray.

We used these responses to generate the coaster design shown above.
